Dr. Himanshu Grover

Research Associate in Astrophysics @ Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee

Research Associate, NCRA–TIFR Pune • Ph.D. in Physics (IIT Roorkee, 2025)

I am a pulsar astronomer investigating the extreme physics of rotating neutron stars and nanohertz gravitational waves. My research bridges radio & X-ray pulsar observations (uGMRT, ORT, AstroSat), high-precision pulsar timing arrays (InPTA / IPTA), pulsar timing irregularities, and Bayesian modeling.

Research Focus & Service

Core Research Interests

  • Radio pulsar observations and high-precision timing arrays.
  • Investigation of pulsar timing irregularities and neutron star interior physics.
  • Pulse profile analysis and Bayesian modelling methodologies.
  • Application of machine-learning methods in astrophysics.
  • Stochastic gravitational wave background searches (InPTA Data Release III).

Professional Service

  • Peer Reviewer: Referee for The Astrophysical Journal and Publications of the Astronomical Society of Australia.
  • Proposal Reviewer: Evaluated observation proposals for the upgraded Giant Metre-wave Radio Telescope (uGMRT).
  • Principal Investigator: Led 17 observation proposals for the GMRT, including target of opportunity high-cadence monitoring for frequent glitching pulsars.
