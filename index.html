Dr. Himanshu Grover

Research Associate @ Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee

Ph.D. in Physics (IIT Roorkee, 2025)

My research focuses on high-precision timing, neutron-star interior physics, and low-frequency gravitational-wave detection within Pulsar Timing Array collaborations (InPTA/IPTA). Combining multi-wavelength observations (uGMRT, ORT, AstroSat) with Bayesian inference and machine-learning frameworks, I develop automated pipelines to model rotational irregularities, isolate interstellar medium effects, and scale precision data analysis for next-generation timing arrays.
